CHAPTER 4RESULTS AND DISCUSSION Performance Evaluation of BFS and DFS Algorithms, together with a proposed Hybrid BFS-DFS approach, in Multi-Exit Dynamic Environments.Results and discussion are presented in this chapter based on the research questions posed in Chapter I related to the performance, adaptability, and interpretability of pathfinding algorithms in conditions other than static grid-based conditions. Outcomes of using and testing the BFS, DFS, and the Hybrid BFS-DFS algorithm are discussed within the web-based comparative visualization system developed by researchers in accordance with the methodology mentioned in Chapter III.The chapter is structured around the four research queries defined earlier in the Statement of the Problem. In this chapter, the results of the real-world procedural representation and dynamic scenario generation methods to create the baseline multi-exit environments are presented at the outset. Next, the comparative outcomes of BFS, DFS, and the Hybrid BFS-DFS algorithm across five real-world scenario domains (Network Routing, Robotics/Warehouse, Road Traffic, Emergency Evacuation, and Game AI Pathfinding) are shown as analyzed against the six main performance evaluation criteria, including execution time, number of nodes visited, optimality, memory usage, completion rate, and adaptability score. Descriptive statistics and One-Way Analysis of Variance (ANOVA) methods are used to reveal the statistical significance of the differences between the three algorithms, with post hoc tests conducted where applicable.This chapter continues with the presentation of results from the acceptability assessment obtained from computer science students in order to assess the comparative visualization system for overall functionality, usability, effectiveness and visualization clarity. Additionally, the chapter offers results of the technical evaluation performed with the help of information technology experts and taking into account the design of the system, reliability, effectiveness of algorithm visualization, and system performance. To summarize, the data obtained in this chapter support the conclusion which shows that all the algorithms react under the influence of dynamic changes in the environment and that Hybrid BFS-DFS algorithm is regarded as a better alternative to the basic uninformed search algorithm. 	Performance Efficiency Assessment of the Proposed System by IT ExpertsTime BehaviorTable
